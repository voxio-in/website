## Basics
- **Name:** Subodh Public School, Rambagh Crossing, Bhawani Singh Marg, Jaipur 302015.
- **Type:** Senior secondary, English medium, co-educational, CBSE. Affiliation No. **1730042**, School Code **10439**.
- **Run by:** Shri S.S. Jain Subodh Shiksha Samiti (founded 1918; it also runs S.S. Jain Subodh PG College).
- **History:** Founded **1985** with classes VI and up. Primary added 1992–93; co-ed since 1995–96. The site says "Celebrating 41 years".
- **Sister school:** Subodh Public School Airport, Sanganer (affiliation 1730280, founded 2004). A different school with a different principal and fees.
- **Size:** 2700+ students. About 123 teachers per the disclosure (PGT 23, TGT 28, PRT 71); the site also says "125+" and "140".

  - German lab (72)
