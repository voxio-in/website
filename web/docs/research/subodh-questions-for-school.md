# Subodh Public School, Rambagh: information needed for the AI calling agent pilot

**From:** VoxioAgents  
**For:** Subodh Public School, Rambagh, Jaipur  
**Date:** October 2026

## Why this document

To prepare our AI calling agent, we read everything published by the school: every page of subodhpublicschool.com, the class timetables, the activity calendar, the fee chart, book lists, circulars, the mandatory disclosure, and the admission poster.

The agent already knows a lot from these, including:
- fees and instalments
- age criteria
- Class XI streams and subjects
- facilities and labs
- board results
- contact details

This document lists what we could not find, and where the published information contradicts itself. Until we have your answers, the agent tells callers that the admissions office will confirm these points.

**Please reply in writing.** Where a list or circular already exists (a documents list, a fee chart, a calendar), a photo or PDF of it is perfect.

## Part 1: Information missing from the website

**1. School timings**
- The 2026-27 timetables (dated 1 April 2026) show **7:40 am to 1:30 pm, Monday to Saturday**, but the activity calendar shows different summer timings.
- What are the timings now, and in winter?
- Are they the same for Nursery, KG and Prep as for Classes I to XII?

**2. Documents required for admission**
- What documents should parents bring? For example: birth certificate, Aadhaar, photographs, previous report card, transfer certificate.
- Is anything different for Class XI, or for students coming from another board or state?

**3. Admission process for 2027-28**
- What are the last dates to register?
- What does the interaction or assessment involve for each class, and when is it held?
- When and how are results shared?
- Is there a waiting list if seats are full?
- Is mid-session admission possible, and up to which class?

**4. Fees: details beyond the fee chart**
- Will the 2027-28 fees be the same as 2026-27, and when will the new chart be released?
- Are books, uniform, exams or activities charged separately, and roughly how much?

**5. Concessions and scholarships**

These appear only on the admission poster, without details:
- **Class XI merit-cum-need scholarship:** is it open to students from other schools? What does "need" mean? Does it apply to tuition only or the full fee? Does it continue in Class XII?
- **Jain student concession:** how much, and what proof is required?
- **Sibling discount:** how much?
- **One-time fee payment rebate:** how much?
- **Defence and martyrs' wards (25% of tuition):** what proof is required?
- May the agent share these amounts with callers, or should it only say that concessions are available?

**6. Uniform and books**
- What is the uniform for each stage, and where do parents buy it?
- Where do parents buy books? The website has book lists only for Classes I to VIII.

**7. Other common questions**
- Roughly how many students are there in a class?
- Is there day care, or a meal or cafeteria plan?
- Are NCC, the sports academies, MUN and the exchange programmes included in the fees, or charged separately?
- What should the agent tell a parent whose child has special needs, and whom should they contact?
- Can parents visit the campus before applying? On which days, and by appointment or walk-in?
- Class XI: which subject combinations are allowed, and is there a cut-off for Science?

**8. Contact numbers**
- Which number should the agent give for admissions, fees and general queries, and during what hours?
- The website and poster list 0141-2568477, 2572426, 9119128646 (WhatsApp), 9414066126, 9413444099 and 9414045483.

## Part 3: Topics deliberately left out

We have **not** given the agent any information on:
- **Bus routes, stops, transport timings and charges**
- **SC/ST and other category or reservation questions**

These are left out on purpose, to test how the agent handles questions it has no information on. It should acknowledge the question politely, never make anything up, and steer the conversation back to how it can help. **You do not need to send us anything on these topics.** Please do ask about them during the pilot.
